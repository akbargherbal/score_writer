# Template for `VAR_REPORT.md` (one per song, written in simple Arabic)

The reader has no music education and will test the variants by ear later. Put any music term in English
in parentheses and explain it in one everyday sentence the first time. Do not claim anything "sounds
better". Say what changed and what to listen for. Keep the whole file under about 60 lines.

The report is also his record of *what was preserved*: if a later track comes out worse, this page is where he
checks whether the seed had already lost something.
